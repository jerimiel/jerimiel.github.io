About me

I started out in mechanical engineering at Landmark University, then did my M.Sc. in Mechatronics Engineering at Ashesi University and ETH Zürich. Somewhere along the way, designing parts stopped being enough. I wanted them to move, sense and react.

Most of my projects run the same loop: model it, design it in Fusion 360, print or build it, write the embedded code and controllers, then fix whatever the real hardware disagrees with. That loop has covered a 3D-printed robot pickup arm, a self-balancing robot and a sensor enclosure, and one much bigger machine: a 4,000 t/d cement kiln at Holcim Maroc, where I worked on its high-level control system.

I'm happiest where mechanical design, robotics and control meet on real hardware. Away from the bench, you'll usually find me watching movies, especially old classics and science fiction.
