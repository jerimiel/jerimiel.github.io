Air-Quality Monitor Enclosure

Product prototype · June 2026

Fusion 3603D printingEnclosure designLow-power firmware

Overview

I designed and prototyped a custom enclosure for an air-quality monitoring device, working with the product developer to turn the device's technical requirements into a functional, printable part.

Design

I modelled the enclosure in Fusion 360 and produced it by 3D printing. The printed part has a perforated top panel and side vent slots for airflow to the sensor, and cut-outs for the device's ports.
