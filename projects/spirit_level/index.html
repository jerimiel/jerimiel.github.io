Electronic Spirit Level

Embedded Systems course · Ashesi University · May – June 2024

MSP432Embedded CAccelerometer

Overview

For my embedded systems and programming course, I used the MSP432 development board and an accelerometer to build an electronic spirit level that measures how level a surface is with very little error.

Why

A battery-powered spirit level that fits in a pocket is more practical than the mechanical ones most people use: it is cheaper and takes up far less space.
